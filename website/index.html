⚡ Features

Everything Your Hostel Needs

From daily meals to monthly expense settlements — AIFDMS replaces paper registers, WhatsApp groups, and manual tracking with one beautiful app.

🍽️
Smart Meal System
Mark daily meal attendance, view weekly menus set by the warden, and rate food quality to give direct feedback to the kitchen.
💸
Bill Splitting
Split dinner bills, grocery runs, or any group expense equally, by percentage, or custom amounts with double-shake settlement verification.
📷
Photo Complaints
Snap a photo of broken fixtures, leaking taps, or dirty common areas. Track resolution status with live warden updates.
📢
Push Notifications
Receive instant alerts for warden notices, leave approvals, meal schedule changes, birthday wishes, and emergency announcements.
📋
Attendance Tracking
Wardens mark daily hostel attendance, generate weekly/monthly reports, and monitor student presence patterns over time.
💬
In-App Chat
Students and wardens communicate through private messaging channels. Share images, documents, and updates without sharing personal numbers.
🏖️
Leave Management
Students submit leave requests digitally with reason and dates. Wardens approve or decline with a single tap and optional notes.
📊
Budget Tracker
Set a monthly spending limit and track personal and shared expenses with visual progress meters, pie charts, and analytics.

📲 Installation

Up & Running in 3 Steps

No Play Store needed. Download and install directly from this page in under a minute.

1
Download the APK
Tap the "Download for Android" button. The APK file will be saved to your phone's Downloads folder.
2
Allow Installation
Open the downloaded file. If prompted, tap Settings → Allow from this source, then tap Install.
3
Sign In & Go
Launch the app, register with your hostel email, verify your account, and you're all set!
