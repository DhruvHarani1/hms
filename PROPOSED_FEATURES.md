# AIFDMS Hostel App — Proposed Feature Roadmap

This document outlines high-value, practical, and standard administrative features that improve the core operations of a student PG/hostel.

---

## 📄 1. Digital Rent Ledger & HRA Receipts
*Target: Student, Warden*

### A. Monthly Rent Card & Dues Tracker
* **Description**: A simplified ledger screen showing:
  * Current month's rent status (Paid / Pending / Overdue).
  * Breakdown of charges (Room Rent + Mess Fee + Electricity unit charges).
  * History of past transactions.

### B. PDF Receipt Downloader (HRA Claims)
* **Description**: Once rent is verified as paid by the warden, the student can download a digitally signed rent receipt PDF directly from the app to use for tax (HRA) declarations.

---

## 🚪 2. Curfew Extensions & Late Entry Requests
*Target: Student, Warden*

### A. Late-In Permission
* **Description**: A student can request an extension to the PG curfew time (e.g., return at 11:00 PM instead of 10:00 PM due to college projects, exams, or late office shifts).
* **Warden Dashboard**: Wardens receive a push alert to quickly approve or decline. Once approved, the system updates a live status board showing who is expected late.

---

## 📷 3. Photo-Attached Maintenance Tickets
*Target: Student, Warden*

### A. Visual Complaint Submissions
* **Description**: When creating a complaint (e.g. leaking flush, broken cupboard, AC remote not working), students can attach a photo directly from their camera.
* **Impact**: Eliminates guesswork. The warden or handyman knows exactly what spare parts to bring before visiting the student's room.

---

## 🍽️ 4. Sick Meal Request (Custom Kitchen Prep)
*Target: Student, Cook*

### A. Light Diet Option
* **Description**: When a student is unwell and cannot eat the regular spicy PG meal, they can toggle a "Sick Meal" option in the app (e.g., Khichdi, Oats, or Curd Rice).
* **Cook Notification**: The cook sees a separate headcount showing: *25 Regular Lunches, 2 Sick Meals (Khichdi)*.

---

## 📢 5. Critical Notices with Read Acknowledgement
*Target: Student, Warden*

### A. Broadcast Alerts
* **Description**: For urgent announcements (e.g., power cuts, scheduled repairs, water tank cleanups), the warden can post a "Critical Notice" which displays as a high-visibility banner at the top of the student's home feed.
* **Read Receipts**: Students tap "Acknowledge" to dismiss, giving the warden a clean count of who has read the announcement.
